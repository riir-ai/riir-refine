cloudflare bill — reconciliation (billing period, account-wide)

billed = ceil(max(0, period_total − included) / block) × price. The included allowance is monthly and account-wide, so no per-app, per-namespace or per-day figure on this page is a billing quantity — those are marginal costs (what a slice adds once the account is past the allowance). KV is sold by the million and the remainder rounds up: 301.97k billable list ops cost a whole $5.00 block, not $1.51. Cross-checked against the transcribed bill in .benchmarks/cf_bill_pin.json (Issue 065 / Bench 067).
