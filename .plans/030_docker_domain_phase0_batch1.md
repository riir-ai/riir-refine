Three measured facts drove the design, none of them documented upstream: `--progress=rawjson` does NOT carry check results (text is the only interface); findings go to stdout while build errors go to stderr; and **a resolve failure silently truncates the finding set** while still exiting 1 — hence `CheckRun::Errored` discards a partial result rather than reporting a subset.
