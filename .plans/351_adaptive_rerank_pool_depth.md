## Goal

Replace the fixed selection-pool depth (`RERANK_POOL = 37`, hand-repinned 12→16→20→37) and the seat consult's whole-pool submission with a **per-campaign calibrated depth**: a DCG-discounted instantiation-evidence predictor over the pool (`inst_dcg`), percentile-calibrated against per-span oracle depths with a quality-parity operating point. Proof path (pre-registered): on held-out spans, mean predicted depth ≤ 12 (≤ ⅓ of 37) at top-1 parity (paired non-inferiority); seat pick-parity at ≤ ⅓ mean submitted pool. FAIL anywhere → recorded negative, feature stays off, `RERANK_POOL` untouched (the `pool_depth_rescue_guard` keeps its current meaning). Mechanism class is published (Ganguly '23, AcuRank, ToolRerank); the claim is the transfer: deterministic proposer-instantiation evidence plays the LLM judge's role at zero inference-time judge cost.

## Phase 1 — Predictor + falsification arm + calibration

### Tasks

- [ ] **T1.1 `inst_dcg` predictor (FIRST-STAGE order — load-bearing).** New module `src/draft/adaptive_pool_depth.rs`: `inst_dcg(first_stage_pool, k) -> f32` = `Σ_{i≤k} fires(rule_i) / log2(i+1)` over the **pre-rerank similarity order**, top-k only (k = 8 = `HEADROOM_DEPTHS[0]`). ⛔ Computing it over the instantiation-RERANKED order is FORBIDDEN and pinned as a test: the rerank sorts `instantiates` first (`latent_matcher.rs` `all_rules`), so firing rules fill positions 1..m and the discounted sum is a monotone function of m — Spearman-identical to the raw `instantiating` count, i.e. the positional discount would carry zero information. The first-stage similarity order is already paid for by retrieval (no extra walk); the predictor needs `fires()` for its top-k only (k visitor walks; the span parsed once per the module's own cost law). Zero-alloc (scratch `&mut`, `with_capacity` once); deterministic (no RNG). Normalization constant pinned in-module with a reason comment.
- [ ] **T1.2 Baselines + falsification arm.** On the SAME Phase-0 populations, implement and run ALL of: (a) the QPP-family baselines the paper predicts will fail — `pool_spread` (jury_disagreement's), pool-score variance, `rerank_margin`; (b) the raw `instantiating` count (blocks a false "inst_dcg separates" claim that is really the count separating); (c) the **fixed-constant null arm** — depth fixed at the same MEAN depth the adaptive policy realizes (first candidate: 12; measured mean may move it) — because if `d*` is mostly small, "adaptive" may only mean "the constant is too big", and the honest outcomes then are a repin or a keep, not adaptivity. Report per-predictor Spearman(φ, d*(span)) + the parity-operating-point depth each achieves. **Falsification arm:** the paper's negative result says the QPP family reaches parity only at near-full depth. If `inst_dcg` does NOT beat the raw count AND the fixed-constant null at equal mean depth, the assessed-quality transfer is refuted on our evidence — close the plan, keep the note's observations.
- [ ] **T1.3 Percentile depth calibration (pre-registration pinned).** Port the paper's recipe: equal-frequency bins of φ over **K = 5** folds of train spans → per-bin depth = p-th percentile of that bin's `d*`; sweep p to the smallest operating point with `E[top-1(d̂)] ≥ E[top-1(37)]` on train (parity); convert to a `thr_min` in φ-space for inference. **Refuse-to-calibrate floors (written rules, not prose):** ≥ **8 spans per bin** (merge adjacent bins below it; if merging cannot reach 8, refuse calibration and record underpowered); ≥ **40 binned spans** total per fold (unreachable spans excluded per T0.1's definition, never padded back in). Pure percentile arithmetic — no sigmoid/softmax, no learned state; the calibration table is serialized as a pinned artifact (deterministic re-runs, byte-stable) beside the traj store.
