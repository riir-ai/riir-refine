- [x] T3 Escape (kick) semantics do NOT transfer (fixes are discrete edits, not latent states) — only trap *detection* is in scope here; record the delta in the closing note.
  - **CONFIRMED 2026-09-12, and the delta is WIDER than the ticket assumed.** The header claimed T2's detector "can consume the gate's observables (oscillation streak + flip-rate EMA) instead of a bespoke two-way counter". Read against the landed kernel (`katgpt-rs/crates/katgpt-core/src/saddle_escape.rs`, 850 lines, feature `saddle_escape`), **that consumption is NOT viable**, at four layers:
  - **(1) Gate level — blocking.** `SaddleEscapeGate::decide` never reaches its trap classifier unless the wrapped `GainCostLoopHalter` first returns `Halt { reason: Oscillation }` (`saddle_escape.rs:396-404`), and that halter's oscillation test is `cos θ < 0` between successive **update-direction vectors in a continuous latent space**. `TrapObservables` demands `{ gain, cost, cos_theta, step_norm, state_bytes }`. A fix attempt is a discrete span replacement: there is no update direction, no step norm, no state vector, and **no definition of `cos_theta` to supply**. Feeding the gate would mean synthesising one — i.e. writing the bespoke counter the fusion was meant to replace, then laundering it through a kernel.
  - **(2) Statistic level — polarity inversion. This is the real finding.** Even the separable half, `FlipDetector`, measures the wrong quantity. Its indicator is `k != prev_key` (`saddle_escape.rs:192-200`) — a **first-order difference** EMA: "how often does the decoded answer CHANGE". For a latent reasoner a high change rate = trapped. For the healer the **opposite** holds: a high `(code, span_hash)` change rate is *healthy progress* — measured, the 20 depth-5 MULTI-5 runs change key on every one of 5 attempts and go `clean` 20/20, which `FlipDetector` would score at flip rate 1.0 and classify as maximally trapped. The healer's trap signature is **recurrence** (a key RETURNING after an intervening different key), and a first-order-difference EMA is **structurally blind** to it: `A→B→A` and `A→B→C` produce an identical flip rate of 1.0. Not a `flip_tau` retune — a different statistic (a windowed return-map / recurrence detector), which is what `examples/fixseq_prevalence.rs::recurrences` implements and what its canary distinguishes.
  - **(3) Kick level — the original T3 verdict, confirmed.** `apply_kick(state: &mut [f32], seed, eps)` perturbs a contiguous f32 latent by `eps · unit(BLAKE3(seed))` under a geometric `eps_decay` anneal. The healer's state is a **source file plus a suppression set**; there is no metric space in which a bounded perturbation of magnitude `eps` is meaningful and no anneal schedule for discrete edits. `eps0` / `eps_decay` / `state_bytes` have **no image**. The nearest analogue — candidate rotation (skip the argmax, take the k-th) — is (a) already what `tried` does, deterministically and exhaustively, and (b) a re-selection over a finite set, not a perturbation of a state.
  - **(4) Verdict level — the expressiveness already ships.** The gate's actual contribution over two-way halters is the third outcome `HaltOutcome::Trapped { flip_rate, kicks_used }`. The healer's analogue already exists and is not a trap verdict: `FixCompileReport { clean, remaining, iterations, attempts }` — `remaining > 0` is already the honest "could not finish", and `iterations == max_iters` already separates budget exhaustion from candidate exhaustion.
  - **Dependency cost, for the record:** consuming the kernel would have been *cheap* structurally — `katgpt-core` is already riir-clippy's only default dep (BOUNDARY.md), needing only `saddle_escape` added to the feature list (which implies `gain_cost_halt`). **The blocker is semantic, not structural** — which is exactly why it had to be read rather than inferred from the dep graph.
  - **Net delta vs the 2026-09-11 header claim:** the header over-claimed. Neither the Kick (already known) **nor the flip-rate EMA** (new) transfers. What DOES transfer from Research 546 is the *framing question* — "is this loop circling a near-miss?" — and Bench 087 answers it for this driver: no, and it cannot. The paper's *other* half — "commits a nearly-correct answer" — DOES have a live target here, but it is a repair-QUALITY problem (`keep iff count decreases` commits divergent repairs: E0597 42/42, E0499 26/26 diverged) that the floor harness already measures and that no flip counter can address.
