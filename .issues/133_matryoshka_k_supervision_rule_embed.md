## The idea

`rule_embed_poc` (Issue 126 lane (a), Bench 099) trains the span↔rule two-tower scorer against a fixed objective; the shipped suggest lane consumes top-k at whatever slate size the caller picks. The MAttr distill's transferable recipe: **randomize the budget k during training** (`k ~ Uniform` / `log` / `log_both` over slate sizes) and merge through `exact_mass_admit` (the exact-mass gate: `Σσ = k` via bisection — consuming katgpt-rs Issue 879's primitive, landed 2026-09-24) so **one training run yields a ranking calibrated at every slate size** — the whole top-k-accuracy-vs-k curve from one run, instead of a ranking implicitly optimized for one operating point.

Why it matters here: the healer's slate consumers run at different widths (`counter_anchored_slate` k=5 today; fix-lane dispatch has no width constraint), and the withheld-rule anti-memorization read (Bench 099's rule-level BLAKE3 split) would extend naturally to a withheld-WIDTH read (held-out k values) — a cheap extra generalization axis for the reopen gate.
