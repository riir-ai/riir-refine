## TL;DR

The paper is an **audit, not a method**: across 4 evolution recipes × 2 harnesses × 2 benchmarks (16 lineages, GPT-5.4), it measures whether benchmark-score gains survive as reliability, coverage, cost, and transfer — then converts the findings into operator revisions (EvoAudit → AGE). Headline results: **every one of the 16 evolved champions both gains and loses covered tasks** ("coverage is a flow"); 8/11 reliability-improving champions cost MORE per trial; the **highest-scoring parent never yields the largest mean subsequent improvement** in any pool; **stricter admission discards useful alternatives** (a constrained gate — strict improvement + a 0.05 regression cap — retains the reporting-panel best in only 6/15 frozen pools, and its size-normalized score can RISE purely by shrinking the retained set to `{Seed}` — the strong-fallback illusion); transfer is target-dependent (coverage falls 7/8 on the general-task target, rises 7/8 on the coding target). AGE's four revisions — regression-aware select tie-breaks, trace-grounded analyze, dependency-closed mutate, **score-first cost-aware gate** (accept iff score improves, or equal score ∧ comparable ∧ cheaper) — beat the reference method in all four settings.

**Verdict: Gain (small, modelless track — the loop and every cousin run in-process, zero GPU).** The workspace's self-evolve stack is on the RIGHT side of every headline finding, mostly already shipped: the preservation-aware paired scorer **ships as `katgpt-core::adoption_gate`** (Plan 633: `score = (1−α)·retained + α·(1−repeated)`, decline-first ties, `NoEvidence` → keep incumbent; consumers in riir-refine active-set/seat-swap + riir-instinct swap seat with typed `swap_regret`) — landed 2026-11-08 from the AGCL lead (Research 657 §A5), three days after this paper hit arXiv — INDEPENDENT BY LINEAGE (AGCL), not by date. Cost-aware admission ships on the decision family as rethink's `esc_rho` predicate (`adopt(cheap) ⇔ cheap_success ∧ (¬think_success ∨ cheap_cost ≤ ρ·think_cost)`, Issue 064, opt-in, NOT promoted). The turnover LOSS half ships as `frontier_report` contribution-fade. And the paper's frozen-pool admission replay **has already been run here three times** (Bench 148/149/150 — the G3 cells: an α-flip census mechanically classified with exact interior crossover points α* ∈ {0.4958, 0.8298, 0.8654, 0.9164}, a derived-and-confirmed α=0 endpoint identity, zero prediction mismatches across a 48×11 grid). The ONE gap worth a file: the next G3 cell's pre-registered evaluation frame should adopt the paper's D.4 reporting trinity — **best-retention hit × retained-set/window mass × candidate identity, reported jointly** — because our own open Bench-150 Bar-C finding (15/20 demote refusals on accepted-carrying rules showing verdict stability below the 0.90 noise bar on thin 1-row windows) is the measured PRECONDITION for this paper's 'admission discards useful alternatives' class — the refusals themselves PROTECT productive rules; the failure is their noise-fragility, under which a protective refusal can flip to a demote of a productive rule — and the paper's central lesson is that gate quality is NOT adjudicable on any single axis. ## 5. Doctrine corroboration (record-only; per §1.55 "validates our design" is not actionable)

Three of the paper's principles are published support for decisions this workspace already made and measured:

1. **Decline-first ties + permissive-with-paired-scoring > hard caps.** The paper's constrained-gate result (F7) is the failure mode the adoption gate's design avoided: the α-mixed paired scorer + decline-first ties never hard-caps, and issue 994's decline-first tie rule was confirmed on real data where the paper's tie class (small integer panels) does not arise (f64 scores, zero exact ties in the 8×34 census — T3.2 bar 2).
2. **"Evidence must match the decision."** The stack's split is already decision-matched: rate evidence → the rate guard; value evidence → esc_voi; cost evidence → esc_rho; retention evidence → the adoption windows; credit evidence → tier_guard/Elo. The paper is the strongest published statement of the principle behind that split.
3. **"Feedback can be narrower than a change's effects."** This is the oracle-vs-corpus gap the strata/`--corpus-delta`/`coverage_gap_event` lanes measure. The paper's D.4 trinity adds: the EVALUATION of any gate on such feedback must be multi-axis — which is Issue 243's content (and our thin-window fragility datum, Bar C, is the live instance of why: fragile verdicts on narrow feedback are how the paper's discard class would arise here).
