## What it is

~8k lines of Ascend C (Bisheng JIT) achieving 99.8% of HW limit on dense GEMM / 98% MegaMoE on Ascend 950DT. Architecture: persistent kernels over a cube(AIC)/vector(AIV) coroutine split (`__mix__(1, kDirectStore ? 0 : 2)`), explicit gm→L1→L0A/B→L0C memory hierarchy with stage rings + pipe events, fractal (NZ) layouts, MX scaling factors (UE8M0 pairs packed int16, MN-major) riding their own pipeline, and a host-side ANALYTICAL cost model that derives all tile/stage/L2-hint configs from a measured HWDesc rate table.

## Ranked candidates (post-dedup)

DISTINCT-FROM: `proven-redundant-operand-load-elision` (B181: load-time CONTENT proof between two different operands, host-cached — this is same-tensor address identity, in-kernel, no proof needed); `dequant-once-macrotile-stage-reuse-across-microtiles` (B186: intra-block staging amortization — this is CROSS-block load elision); `verify-fold-gqa-repeats-into-row-axis-tile-fill` (B182: restructure the layout — this detects at runtime); `stream-invariant-operand-skips-staging-pipeline` (loop-invariant operand EXCLUDED from the pipeline — this operand ADVANCES, then replays).
