## The three-layer find (why this grades B+ and not C)

The repo's estimators are ordinary; what is exceptional is the **exactness machinery** wrapped around its fast paths, in three composable layers, each with in-repo proof docs:

1. **Certified approximate prefilter** (`_neighbors_screen.py`, `_neighbors_integer.py`, `docs/knn-compact-certificate.md`, `docs/knn-integer-certificate.md`) — a lossy fast pass (fp32 ranking, or int8 quantized codes via Metal-4 integer tensor ops) produces per-partition shortlists PLUS a *next-omitted barrier* (`omitted = min(omitted, max(distance, distances[K-1]))`, emitted by the scan and carried through the merge). The finish kernel ADMITS the fast result only when a bound certificate proves it equals the exact result under conservative f32 interval arithmetic (`precise_distance_lower/upper`); otherwise it escalates — and the escalation granularity is PER ROW: only certificate-uncertain rows run the complete exact scan. Unsafe rows (nonfinite or oversized quantization error) get a **negative-infinity bias so the certificate can never discard them** (refuse-safe marking: the cheap filter defaults to ADMIT, the exact path still decides). Shortlist widening under centering (`carry = k+11 → k+19`) is explicitly a perf knob while "the unchanged omitted-candidate bound remains the acceptance criterion", and `carry <= 32` is "a correctness requirement, not only a tuning knob" (refinement consumes one candidate per lane).
2. **Escalating-precision exact ordering** (`_neighbors_precision.py` `PRECISION_HEADER`) — a three-tier comparison ladder for f32 distance ORDERING: conservative f32 intervals → guarded double-single filter → **exact dyadic integer superaccumulator** (19 signed two's-complement limbs; products of bit-decoded mantissas shifted by exponent deltas; subnormals decoded from bits because FP ops may flush). "Returned distances remain FP32; these helpers only choose their corresponding training indices." True ties resolve by index order only.
3. **Device compensated arithmetic** (`_compensated.py`) — double-float (hi/lo two-float) arithmetic in Metal f32 for cancellation-heavy reductions (large feature offsets): `rn` fast2sum, `add2` (two-sum error recovery), `mul2` (FMA-based error term), `div2`, pairwise `sum2` via `simd_shuffle_down`, a compensated `exp2x` with range reduction. The module docstring states the honest posture verbatim: "The transcendental approximation and arithmetic do not provide a formal error bound; callers must treat the output as a numerical check, not a proof." The clamped exp tail is BOUND, not hidden: `exp2x` clamps at −80 and the kernel emits a per-row `tail_error` the caller sums into a reported bound.

Plus a dispatch layer with measured crossovers wired as two-condition gates (`config.py` `Tuning`: "A problem is 'below the crossover' only if it is small on *both* counts"), a decisive-axis routing law (SVC: "the axis that matters is *width*, not height … rbf at 4000×32 is 0.47×, and the same n at 4000×256 is 2.93×. Both conditions must hold … the linear kernel is excluded at every size"), a cached capability probe that VERIFIES the probe's OUTPUT (`all(value == 32 …)` — never mere absence of error; unsupported hosts retain the incumbent filter), and a data-shaped concentration probe (participation ratio from eight sampled row differences, cached per fit) whose doc explicitly refuses to delegate correctness to the heuristic: "a performance heuristic, not part of either numerical certificate: sampling can miss heterogeneous training data, and either selected filter must still certify its candidates or execute its complete fallback."
