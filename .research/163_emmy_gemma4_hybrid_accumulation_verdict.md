## The measured core (blog = quote authority)

1. **Hybrid FP16/FP32 accumulation (the lead).** Consumer dies (Ada AND Blackwell) run FP16-input/FP32-accumulate HMMA at exactly HALF the FP16-accumulate rate (Ada: 330.3 vs 165.2 peak TFLOPS; Blackwell: 419 vs 209.5). `EMMY_FAST_MATH` spells `mma.sync.aligned.m16n8k16.row.col.f16.f16.f16.f16` (C/D = 2 packed f16x2 regs, 2× the mma-chain rate) and folds the f16x2 partials into an FP32 shadow every **64 K-elements** (`emmy_mma_promote_f16acc`: unpack `mov.b32 {lo,hi}`, `cvt.f32.f16` both lanes, add into the f32 shadow, rezero the f16 accumulator). Error (relative L2 vs FP64 over identical FP16-rounded operands — isolates ACCUMULATION error): FP32-accum 9.2e-8 → 8.1e-7 (grows with K), pure FP16 6.0e-4 → 6.6e-3 (grows with K; worst element ~3.7% at K=15360), **hybrid FLAT ~3.3e-4 at every K** — inside FP16's own representational noise. Measured kernel wins: 1.24–1.55× on top of Emmy's own FP32 lane; 1.34–1.61× vs cuBLAS. GSM8K (200 q, lm-eval, same seed): all lanes within one SE. Provenance chain the article cites: DeepGEMM periodic promotion (FP8/Hopper, every N_C=128), CUTLASS `fp8_accumulation.hpp`, Ootomo & Yokota (hi/lo input split — a DIFFERENT fix for input precision), Blanchard et al. (blocked mixed-precision FMA: error constant γ_{K/b} not γ_K — this scheme is an instance), Patterson 2025 (the same out-of-mma promotion hand-written for ONE 4090 shape). The production negative control: PyTorch's `allow_fp16_accumulation` ships the UNPROTECTED form (all-FP16 accumulation, error grows in K).
2. **TMA vs cp.async, gated by roofline saturation.** cuBLAS consumer-Blackwell GEMMs (FP16 `tensorop` included) are forward-ported Ampere-era `cutlass_80_*` kernels still on `cp.async`; TMA (`cp.async.bulk.tensor` + mbarrier, ONE elected thread fires, every other thread issues NO address arithmetic, TMA swizzle drops bank conflicts for free) wins **1.08–1.26× on shapes BELOW the pipe roof** (q_proj 91%, o_proj 94% of roof) and **1.00–1.02× AT it** (down_proj 211 / gate_up 215 TFLOPS ≈ the 209.5 FP16-acc peak — no bubble for a cheaper transport to fill).
3. **FA at head_dim 256: budget-exhausted single-buffer overlap.** Q+K+V at d256 = 96 KB fills the ~100 KB per-CTA budget — FA-2 runs SINGLE-buffered (no double buffer, no ring) and gets overlap by refilling the one slab at each operand's KILL POINT (next K streams in under softmax + P·V which no longer read K; V overlapped under the following Q·Kᵀ). Emmy's search arrives at the identical schedule. Split-KV (flash-decoding) fires as an OCCUPANCY PATCH: the shape launches 128 CTAs against 170 SMs (profile signature: `flash_fwd_splitkv_kernel` + `flash_fwd_splitkv_combine_kernel`); P·V runs the hybrid accumulation while Q·Kᵀ + softmax statistics stay FP32.
4. **Integration + honesty content.** vLLM plugin boundary costs 0.3–1 ms/token (1–5%): ~1100 launches/step vs stock ~650 (attention seam + unfused norm/stat/scale glue where torch.compile fuses). Decode is memory-bound → kernel optimizations NOT applicable (decode slightly slower than stock). MTP smoke clean (drafts run stock vLLM means; no degradation). One catalog-only fused norm→linear megakernel FAILS the fp16 accuracy check in isolation → serving deploys the cut spelling (fail-closed admission). 4090 (sm_89, no TMA — cp.async stands): geomean 1.06× FAST_MATH vs eager, worst 0.18× (`mlp_down.lin.dynM` — the dynamic-M shape; the tuning-record mirror for later golden rounds is pending → "a floor, not the card's final word"). Reporting: geomean "to prevent outliers"; a TTFT footnote pins the median-inversion trap (a `--num-prompts 64` wave measures prefill under saturation; 256 prompts lands the median on queue-drain, not prefill).
