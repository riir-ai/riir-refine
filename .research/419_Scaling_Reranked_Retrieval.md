## TL;DR

Weaviate measures where the next unit of inference compute buys ranking quality in a retrieve → cross-encode → listwise pipeline over five BRIGHT subsets (512 queries): **DEPTH** (k=100→2,000) nearly doubles pool recall (0.476→0.871) but the reranked head saturates by k=1,000 (nDCG@10 0.423→0.424 flat; S@1 peaks 0.397) while RETENTION keeps converting (R@20 0.418→0.560) — the same law our rulewright depth census measured (384 addendum: deeper pools buy recall insurance, never top-1 recovery), now published cross-domain with a price curve. **STAGES** compose (hybrid 0.181 → +cross-encoder 0.424 → +listwise 0.470 nDCG@10); the cross-encoder sells REACH (94% of spend; distills the 0.871-recall pool into a 0.560-recall top-20), and removing it halves window recall (0.738→0.476), collapsing listwise ordering (0.482→0.375) — no ordering budget substitutes for reach. **WIDTH** is the measured NEGATIVE: ~52 model-unique rank-1 successes per stage (≈10% of queries), yet equal-weight RRF/RSF fusion converts almost none of it (≈ best singleton at the cross-encoder stage; BELOW the best singleton at the listwise stage) — per-query routing, not blending, is the converter. The decision family already ships the routing form (ESC); this note anchors that design in published conversion numbers — and finds the repo already holds the ESC pair's gold-referenced disagreement structure: the Bench-195 record publishes the fired-set 2×2 over the 117 escalated rows (both right 47 · think-only 35 · cheap-only 18 · both wrong 17; oracle ceiling 85.5% vs served 70.1%, the 15.4-pt headroom = exactly the cheap-only rows). The paper is the published external version of that number; the actionable residue (reconciling Issue 122's δ composition against the published 2×2, and printing the ceiling beside its fit bars) is redirected INTO Issue 122.

**Distilled for riir-refine (modelless, inference-time) — three allocation laws for any staged selection pipeline (fan-out → rerank seat → hosted escalation):**

1. **Depth buys POSSIBILITY, priced in distraction.** Pool recall saturates sublinearly; head-of-list gains stop ~an order of magnitude before retention does. `RERANK_POOL`'s re-pin history (12→37) is the membership half, Plan 351's Phase-0 census (d\* constant at 8; unreachable spans depth-invariant) the ranking half — the paper confirms both halves on BRIGHT and prices the tail: the last depth doubling bought +0.001 nDCG@10 for $0.0175/query. Operational caveat theirs, not ours: k=2,000 over token-billed APIs is rate-limited to 1–4 queries/min; our fan-out is in-process µs-scale, so depth is cheap here in latency but still priced in distractor exposure.
2. **A stage's justification is what the previous stage CANNOT do.** Cheap first stage = reach (membership); rerank = ordering the head; listwise/escalation = joint comparison. Removing the reach stage cannot be compensated by more ordering compute — the published stage-removal cell (0.482→0.375) is the general law behind "a rule not in the pool can never be picked" (384 addendum) and behind the fan-out-stays-wide posture (kernel_route_narrow, Bench 126, measured the narrowing negative).
3. **Static width is a floor, not a lever.** Heterogeneous-lane disagreement is real (≈10% of queries per stage here; 26% think-unique on the ESC pair's Bench-195 capture — Issue 122 T1), but equal-weight fusion pays every member everywhere to profit from the few it uniquely solves. The converter is per-query routing — the ESC's form. Boundary note: our positive ESC-composed H2+mammoth board rows are CASCADE composition (cheap gate → specialist on flagged survivors), not equal-weight peer blending; the paper's negative does not transfer to that shape (cite, never merge the two).
