## TL;DR

The stack owns every half of the AlphaZero selection recipe and has never wired them together in the rulewright: **Q** = the incumbent evidence score (`W_EVO·evo + W_RATE·reliability`), **P** = priors from modelless evolution scores and tiny trained heads (`seat_rerank`, Bench 119), **N** = per-candidate outcome counts in the `TrajStore`; and a **generic `PUCBSelector`** (prior + visit-count UCB bonus, `c=0.2`/`K=64` paper defaults) sits in katgpt-pruners with **zero consumers outside katgpt-rs** (grep-verified this session — the goat-audit stall shape). The fusion is `SelectionMode::Puct` in `select_best_candidate`: `score = Q + c·P·√(ΣN)/(1+N)`, whose real content is the **crossover law** — at `c=0` the score IS the incumbent score byte-identically, and as a candidate's own `N` accumulates the bonus decays toward zero, so the prior's influence is largest at thin evidence and the measured record dominates as evidence grows. "Some problems use NN, some use trajectories" is not a routing table; it is a smooth per-candidate crossover, and PUCT is its closed form. **GOAT(fusion)-tier: components published or shipped, composition + framing ours.** Scope honesty: `select_best_candidate` is SINGLE-PICK (one candidate validated per heal; miss → `resolve_miss` → L4 — no ranked retry loop exists, and this filing does not add one); the measured premise is therefore **selection regret over a sequential replay** (Bench 028's harness shape, the gate that promoted `BetaPosterior`), not wall-clock ordering. Two standing priors bound the expectation honestly: the rustc_errors **nonergodic ordering lane is CLOSED NEGATIVE** (Issue 093 — rotation-style exploration ordering), and the **Entropic** pool tilt (Issue 026) shipped without displacing the BetaPosterior default. The cell (Issue 229) is the pre-registered test of whether *prior-weighted count-decayed* selection behaves differently from both — with a prominent demotion law. Decision-family half **veto-gated** (serving law forbids online exploration on the served path; ESC outcome join is receipts-only — no N axis).

## 3. The fusion — what none alone has

1. **The crossover law (the note's durable contribution).** `c·P·√(ΣN)/(1+N)` makes each candidate's selection basis shift from NN-prior to trajectory-statistics as its own evidence grows: a fresh rule/shape with `N=0` is ordered by the head/evolution prior alone; a candidate with 50 outcomes is ordered by its measured reliability with the prior as a tiebreak. The incumbent modes each pick ONE side of this axis globally (`BetaPosterior` = statistics-only with an LCB that under-explores by construction; `seat_rerank` = prior-only with abstention; `Entropic` = prior-only over the pool). None has the per-candidate crossover, and the crossover is what the owner's "some problems use NN, some use trajectories" describes.
2. **The self-evolve loop unification.** P = evolution score / seat-head score; N = `TrajStore` outcome counts; the katgpt-rs Research 648 weight-edit lane = the *repair arm* when Q reveals a systematic named bias (obstacle-law arming); the katgpt-rs Research 655 zoo = the *P-side warm-start* for new suites/rules. One selection grammar over the whole self-evolve stack — filed arms, not new machinery: 648 → katgpt-rs Issues 980/993, 655 → riir-train Plan 487.
3. **Why refine is the right first consumer (and the honest risk).** Selection is the one modelless choice whose outcome is labeled and persisted (`TrajStore`) — and the gate precedent exists: Bench 028 measured **selection regret** over randomized trials to promote BetaPosterior. `select_best_candidate` is SINGLE-PICK (one candidate validated per heal; miss → `resolve_miss` → L4; **no ranked retry loop exists** — adding one is explicitly out of scope here), so the measured premise is NOT wall-clock ordering: it is **regret over a sequential replay** where the store's counts update between episodes — the only way exploration's payoff (later episodes learning from exploratory picks) is observable at all; a frozen store can only measure the cost. The LinearBlend arm separates any gain from BetaPosterior's own documented large-N crossover (+2.9% at 30 attempts, L172-179). The growth driver that could flip the answer: **pools are growing** — `heal_with_pool` (Issue 161) adds the per-lint proposer member + the `rewrite` whole-module merge strategy; the escalation chain adds seat-ranked members. The bonus's edge is pool-size- and evidence-depth-dependent, so the cell stratifies by pool size (n=1, 2–3, ≥4) and pre-registers the expected honest outcome at small n.
