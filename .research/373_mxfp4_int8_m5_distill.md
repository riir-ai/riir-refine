## What the source is

The complete W4A8 system behind the paper: on Apple M5 Neural Accelerators (Metal 4 `matmul2d`, INT8×INT8 at 1.79–1.98× FP16; every mixed float×integer combination SLOWER than FP16), a row of MXFP4 weights is EXACTLY one INT8 vector times one power-of-two scale iff its block exponents span ≤ 3 — so a standard MXFP4 linear layer becomes ONE exact INT8 GEMM with one rescale per output, no per-block work in the reduction. Two formats: **SC-MXFP4** (spread-constrained, stays a standard MXFP4 file, 4.07 bits with the 2-bit-shift packing) and the **integer-exact 16-block family** (integer multipliers 1..10, 4.26 bits, beats NVFP4's standard scale rule, within 0.06 ppl of NVFP4+scale-search on five small models, best GSM8K measured on the 125B MoE). Results: 97.9–99.97% of rows across seven models satisfy the spread condition unmodified (99.93% of the 95.4M rows of a 125B MoE); prefill 1.34–1.74× vs MLX MXFP4 on dense, 1.10–1.57× on MoEs to 35B, 1.56–2.49× vs llama.cpp Q4_0 on the dense model; outputs bit-identical to the quantized reference; decode ~parity with MLX (memory/latency-bound — INT8 arithmetic does not help decode). Layout: `kernels/` (Metal 4 matmul + W4A8, 105/233/31 ln), `sim/` (quantizers + formats, NumPy reference), `e2e/` (MLX-integrated fused prefill + grouped MoE + decode GEMV + correctness/bench scripts). Completeness green: self-tests, per-result reproduction scripts, a GPU-timing lock tool; WIP signals none (a fresh paper repo at v1). Hardware regime: M5-class INT8 matrix units — the workspace owns a 4090 (INT8 dp4a/IMMA tensor cores, ~2× FP16 — the analogous datapath) + M3 Metal (wgpu; NOT the Neural Accelerator path).
