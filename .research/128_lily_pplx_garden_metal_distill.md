## 2. What Lily is

A single-model (Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, MLX affine-4bit, group 64) Metal inference engine: objc2-metal raw bindings (no wgpu/MLX in the execution path), runtime shader compilation, serial compute pass for prefill + `MTLDispatchType::Concurrent` pass for decode, GDN hybrid (30 GDN + 10 GQA layers), 256-expert MoE (8 routed + 1 shared), greedy decode. **Runtime gate: Apple GPU family ≥ 10 (M5+) / macOS 26+** — Metal 4 tensor ops are the production GEMM path (family 9 = M3/M4 would run MPP *emulation*; Lily refuses below family 10). **Read-only mining source on our pinned M3 Max** — the antirez/ds4 precedent (B97/98, M5-Max-targeted, mined read-only); no on-box GOAT measurement of Lily's own numbers is possible. Provenance: portions of `quant.metal` / `moe.metal` / `gdn.metal` / `attention.metal` are **adapted from MLX/MLX-LM (MIT)** — cite MLX as upstream for those constructions; Lily's own contributions are the dispatch/tile geometry and the orchestration layer (which is where the two A- leads live). NOTICE discloses the per-file adaptation map — the cleanest third-party provenance document we have mined.
